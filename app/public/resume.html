Upload an existing resume or start with a simple template. Use only your real education, skills, and experience.

1. Start your draft

Import or replace resume: PDF, Word (.docx), or text (.txt), up to 5 MB

Files are read in this browser. Your resume is not uploaded to the server or sent to job providers. Scanned PDFs need selectable text; older .doc files aren’t supported.

Choosing a file imports its text but does not save it. After importing, choose Save resume on this browser to keep it when you leave.

Jump to resume preview

2. Edit and review

Imported text goes into the resume text box. Check its order and formatting. Move your name and contact details to the optional fields below if you want; remove any duplicates from the text.

Your draft is not saved. Download it before leaving, or choose Save draft on this browser.

3. Download

For PDF, choose Save as PDF in your browser’s print window. Check page breaks before saving. Text import does not preserve the original layout.
