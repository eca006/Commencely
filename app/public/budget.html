Estimate sources and assumptions

Choosing a household fills in food prepared at home, transportation, and rent with most utilities. These are modeled monthly costs for the Fayetteville–Springdale–Rogers metro. Enter your combined household pay above and adjust the costs below as needed. Living Wage Institute, 2026

Starting pay uses the Fayetteville–Springdale–Rogers metro’s median hourly wage of $19.99 across all occupations, multiplied by 2,080 hours for a full-time year. BLS, May 2023

The 25% starting value is an editable planning assumption for all paycheck deductions combined. Use your own percentage if you know it. The estimate spreads annual pay evenly across 12 months.

For comparison, the metro’s overall median gross rent was $1,157 per month, including utilities. Census ACS, 2020–2024

These FY 2027 Fair Market Rents cover the Fayetteville–Springdale–Rogers metro and include most tenant-paid utilities. Edit housing to reflect your own share of rent and utilities. HUD rent schedule

The housing warning uses 30% of gross income as a planning benchmark. HUD housing affordability analysis
