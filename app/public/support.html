Job search, resumes, and interviews

Arkansas Workforce Center — Fayetteville

Get help updating your resume, practicing interviews, finding training, and connecting with employers. Resource rooms offer computers, internet, and printers.

Who it is for: People seeking work. Ask the center about eligibility for additional programs.

Cost: Workforce Center services are free.

Where: 2153 E. Joyce Blvd., Suite 201, Fayetteville, AR 72701.

Next step: Call 479-521-5730 to ask how to get started.

College career guidance

NWACC Career Services

Explore career paths, majors, degrees, and certificates, and find job and internship resources.

Who it is for: NWACC students and alumni. Other visitors should ask which services they can use.

Cost: The linked page does not list a service fee; ask when booking.

Where: Bentonville campus, Becky Paneitz Student Center, room 206. By appointment only.

Next step: Email career_services@nwacc.edu to request an appointment.

Community resources

Arkansas 211, powered by Hark

Get connected to community resources when you need support alongside your job search.

Who it is for: Phone support currently serves Benton, Madison, and Washington counties. People in other Arkansas counties can use the online resource search.

Cost and eligibility: Ask about the requirements and any costs for the service you are referred to.

Next step: Call 211 or 1-866-489-6983, or search Arkansas 211 resources.
