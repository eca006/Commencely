Dress for Success Northwest Arkansas

Professional clothing and accessories for interviews and the first weeks of work, plus mock interviews, resume assistance, and career coaching.

Next step: Call 479-877-6910 to schedule a clothing appointment or ask about career support.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Women seeking support toward economic independence in Northwest Arkansas.

Cost: The linked service pages do not state a fee. Ask when scheduling.

Where: 1200 W. Walnut, Suite 1190, Rogers, AR 72756. Clothing visits are by appointment.

Walton Career Closet — University of Arkansas

Business casual and professional clothing for interviews, career fairs, internships, and work. Clothing is yours to keep.

Next step: Check the current walk-in hours. No appointment is needed during regular fall and spring walk-in hours; summer and university breaks require an appointment.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Currently enrolled Walton College students.

Cost: Free. Up to four individual items per semester and one suit per academic year. Sizes and items depend on donations.

Where: Willard J. Walker Hall, room 106, Fayetteville.

Email wcobcdc@uark.edu · Call 479-575-6100

Samaritan Community Center — Samaritan Care

Clothing vouchers, community resource referrals, and help navigating support programs.

Next step: Call 479-340-0109 or email care@samcc.org to ask about clothing vouchers and an appointment.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Individuals and families facing difficult circumstances. Clothing vouchers for adults and children depend on a needs assessment and qualifying criteria.

Cost: Ask the Care Team about voucher eligibility and any service costs before visiting.

Where: Rogers and Springdale centers. Start with the Care Line rather than going directly to a thrift shop.

Job search, resumes, and interviews

Goodwill Industries of Arkansas — Career Services

Interview preparation, career coaching, resume help, job-search assistance, and job-readiness training.

Next step: Call 501-372-5100, extension 1229, or email CareerServices@GoodwillAR.org to arrange a Career Navigator appointment.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Arkansans seeking career support. Services are available online and in person; ask about the nearest Northwest Arkansas appointment option.

Cost: Career services are free. This listing does not confirm a free-clothing program.

Official Goodwill career services and online portal

NWACC Adult Education

GED preparation, English-language classes, and workforce education. Enrolled students can ask about supportive services for transportation and work necessities.

Next step: Call 479-986-6911 or email askadulted@nwacc.edu to start enrollment.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Adult learners. Contact the program to check enrollment and supportive-service requirements; funding is not automatic.

Cost: Adult Education classes are free. Ask about any testing or other separate expenses.

Getting to interviews and work

Ozark Regional Transit — On-Demand

Shared public transportation within designated service zones. Check that your interview or workplace is inside a zone before booking.

Next step: Call 479-756-5901 or use the ORT On-Demand app to check a trip. Phone booking is available without a smartphone.

Eligibility, costs, and sources

Who it is for: Riders traveling within the covered zones. The current page lists Fayetteville and Rogers Monday–Saturday, and Bentonville and Springdale on Saturdays; hours vary by city.

Cost: The listed fare is $1.25 per trip for a group of up to five riders. Confirm current fares and coverage with ORT.

Official booking instructions, fares, and service-zone map

Checked October 28, 2026. Trip availability is not guaranteed.
