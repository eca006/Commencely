Interview and work clothing

Dress for Success Northwest Arkansas

Help available: Professional clothing and accessories for interviews and the first weeks of work, plus mock interviews, resume assistance, and career coaching.

Who it is for: Women seeking support toward economic independence in Northwest Arkansas.

Cost: The linked service pages do not state a fee. Ask when scheduling.

Where: 1200 W. Walnut, Suite 1190, Rogers, AR 72756. Clothing visits are by appointment.

Next step: Call 479-877-6910 to schedule a clothing appointment or ask about career support.

Walton Career Closet — University of Arkansas

Help available: Business casual and professional clothing for interviews, career fairs, internships, and work. Clothing is yours to keep.

Who it is for: Currently enrolled Walton College students.

Cost: Free. Up to four individual items per semester and one suit per academic year. Sizes and items depend on donations.

Where: Willard J. Walker Hall, room 106, Fayetteville.

Next step: Check the current walk-in hours. No appointment is needed during regular fall and spring walk-in hours; summer and university breaks require an appointment.

Email wcobcdc@uark.edu · Call 479-575-6100

Checked October 28, 2026.

Samaritan Community Center — Samaritan Care

Help available: Clothing vouchers, community resource referrals, and help navigating support programs.

Who it is for: Individuals and families facing difficult circumstances. Clothing vouchers for adults and children depend on a needs assessment and qualifying criteria.

Cost: Ask the Care Team about voucher eligibility and any service costs before visiting.

Where: Rogers and Springdale centers. Start with the Care Line rather than going directly to a thrift shop.

Next step: Call 479-340-0109 or email care@samcc.org to ask about clothing vouchers and an appointment.

Official Samaritan Care services and current walk-in hours

Job search, resumes, and interviews

Goodwill Industries of Arkansas — Career Services

Help available: Interview preparation, career coaching, resume help, job-search assistance, and job-readiness training.

Who it is for: Arkansans seeking career support. Services are available online and in person; ask about the nearest Northwest Arkansas appointment option.

Cost: Career services are free. This listing does not confirm a free-clothing program.

Next step: Call 501-372-5100, extension 1229, or email CareerServices@GoodwillAR.org to arrange a Career Navigator appointment.

Official Goodwill career services and online portal

Checked October 28, 2026.

Arkansas Workforce Center — Fayetteville

Get help updating your resume, practicing interviews, finding training, and connecting with employers. Resource rooms offer computers, internet, and printers.

Who it is for: People seeking work. Ask the center about eligibility for additional programs.

Cost: Workforce Center services are free.

Where: 2153 E. Joyce Blvd., Suite 201, Fayetteville, AR 72701.

Next step: Call 479-521-5730 to ask how to get started.

Official Workforce Center services and locations

Checked October 27, 2026.

Open Avenues — Community Employment

Help available: Job placement, supported employment, personalized career planning, and ongoing workplace support.

Who it is for: People with disabilities in Northwest Arkansas. Open Avenues also participates in Ticket to Work; ask which pathway and eligibility requirements apply to you.

Cost: The program page does not list fees. Ask about funding and any costs when contacting the team.

Where: Open Avenues is based at 2202 N. 24th St., Rogers, AR 72756; employment support connects participants with community jobs.

Next step: Call 479-636-5082 or email icamacho@openavenues.org to ask about Community Employment.

Training and English classes

NWACC Adult Education

Help available: GED preparation, English-language classes, and workforce education. Enrolled students can ask about supportive services for transportation and work necessities.

Who it is for: Adult learners. Contact the program to check enrollment and supportive-service requirements; funding is not automatic.

Cost: Adult Education classes are free. Ask about any testing or other separate expenses.

Where: Shewmaker Center for Workforce Technologies, second floor, NWACC, Bentonville. Ask which class locations and schedules fit your needs.

Next step: Call 479-986-6911 or email askadulted@nwacc.edu to start enrollment.

Official Adult Education programs and supportive services

Cost: Classes and tutoring are free.

Where: 2596 N. Keystone Crossing, Fayetteville, AR 72703, and online.

Getting to interviews and work

Ozark Regional Transit — On-Demand

Help available: Shared public transportation within designated service zones. Check that your interview or workplace is inside a zone before booking.

Who it is for: Riders traveling within the covered zones. The current page lists Fayetteville and Rogers Monday–Saturday, and Bentonville and Springdale on Saturdays; hours vary by city.

Cost: The listed fare is $1.25 per trip for a group of up to five riders. Confirm current fares and coverage with ORT.

Next step: Call 479-756-5901 or use the ORT On-Demand app to check a trip. Phone booking is available without a smartphone.

Official booking instructions, fares, and service-zone map

Checked October 28, 2026. Trip availability is not guaranteed.

Community resources

Arkansas 211, powered by Hark

Get connected to community resources when you need support alongside your job search.

Who it is for: Phone support currently serves Benton, Madison, and Washington counties. People in other Arkansas counties can use the online resource search.

Cost and eligibility: Ask about the requirements and any costs for the service you are referred to.

Next step: Call 211 or 1-866-489-6983, or search Arkansas 211 resources.
