    Confirm your recovery email

    Confirm only if you requested this change. This replaces your recovery address, invalidates older recovery links, and signs out existing sessions.

    Choose a new password

    The email link is not enough by itself. Enter a fresh authenticator or unused recovery code. Your authenticator stays enabled.

    Forgot your password but still have your authenticator?

    Request a link to the recovery email you previously verified in account settings. You will also need your authenticator or an unused recovery code.

    Email recovery is not enabled. Use saved recovery codes below. A sign-in email address is not automatically a verified recovery address.

    Reset using saved recovery codes

    Resetting consumes one code. To sign in afterward, you will still need your authenticator or another unused recovery code. Do not use your last code for this reset.

    Lost access to your authenticator?

    If you know your password, sign in using an unused recovery code. In recovery settings you can replace your remaining codes after entering your password and a fresh code.

    No authenticator or recovery codes?

    Email alone cannot restore access. There is no manual support recovery process. Keep any signed-in device open, but an existing session alone cannot change your security settings. You can still use the public career tools. Browser-stored drafts are not backed up to an online account.
