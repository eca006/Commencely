    Let’s start with your header

    Include your name and ways for employers to reach you.

    About you

    How should an employer contact you? A street address isn’t needed. Leave out any contact detail you don’t want to share.

    Education and training

    What school, course, certificate, or training have you attended? Include something you’re still working toward and say when you expect to finish. You can skip this step.

    What’s your level of education?

    Experience

    Think beyond paid jobs. Have you volunteered, helped a family business, organized an event, cared for someone, or held a campus role? Describe your own role and what you contributed.

    No experience to add yet? Leave this blank and go to the next step.

    Skills

    Select skills you can demonstrate, or write your own. Six to eight relevant skills is a useful starting point.

    Add only skills you have. Include your level when helpful.

    Summary

    Start with an option based on the details you’ve entered, or write your own. You can skip this section.
