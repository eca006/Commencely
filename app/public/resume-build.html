Experience

Projects and skills

A class assignment, personal project, club activity, or community project can show what you’ve practiced. Focus on your own contribution. Skip anything you don’t have yet.

Write one skill per line. Think about software you’ve used, a language you speak, equipment you can operate, or a task you can demonstrate. Add your level when helpful, such as “Excel — basic.”
